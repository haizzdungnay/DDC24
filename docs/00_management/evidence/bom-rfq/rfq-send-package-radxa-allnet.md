## Email 1 — Gửi Radxa

**To:** sales@radxa.com
**Subject:** RFQ: Radxa CM3 (RK3566) Volume Pricing + Extended-Temp Variant — KINEXUS Technologies

```
Dear Radxa Sales Team,

KINEXUS Technologies is developing an industrial DDC (Direct Digital Control)
controller for commercial building HVAC/BMS applications in Southeast Asia,
and we have selected Radxa CM3 as our SOM platform.

Our application requires an extended operating temperature range of -20°C to
+80°C. The standard consumer-grade CM3 (0°C ~ 50°C) does not meet this
requirement.

1. Target Part Identity
   Radxa CM3, RK3566, 2GB LPDDR4 RAM / 8GB eMMC, no WiFi.

2. Volume Pricing Request
   Please provide unit pricing (FOB or EXW) at:
   - Qty 100 — NPI / field trial units
   - Qty 500 — first production batch (our target: ~$30/unit)
   - Qty 1,000 — steady-state quarterly volume

3. Extended-Temperature Variant Availability
   - Does Radxa offer a CM3 variant rated for -20°C to +80°C?
   - If yes: part number, pricing at tiers above, lead time, MOQ.
   - If no: is RK3566 silicon available in industrial-grade die
     (-40°C to +85°C)? Any industrial-grade CM3 on roadmap?

4. Lead Time & MOQ
   Standard lead time at each volume tier, and any MOQ considerations.

5. Screening / Binning
   If no extended-temp CM3 variant exists, does Radxa offer screening/binning
   of consumer-grade units that pass extended-temp validation?

Company: KINEXUS Technologies — Vietnamese engineering company, industrial
IoT and building automation. Product launch target Q4 2026.

Please reply at your earliest convenience. NDA available if required.

Best regards,
[CEO — KINEXUS Technologies]
[CEO to fill in contact details]
```
