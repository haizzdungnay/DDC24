## 3. Channel Strategy Implications

1. **Vietnam home market advantage:** No import duty, lower distributor margin expectations → best margin for KINEXUS AND lowest end-user price
2. **Philippines worst-case:** 12% import duty + 35% distributor margin → end-user price ~960 USD, narrowing advantage vs competitors
3. **Recommendation:** Go direct-to-SI in Vietnam (cut distributor layer) → end-user price ~638 USD at EXW 510
4. **Non-VN markets:** Must use distributor model due to local presence requirements
