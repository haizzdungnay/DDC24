## 2. Bottom-Up TAM Estimation Methodology

### 2.5 SAM & SOM Estimation

**SAM** (Serviceable Addressable Market): Countries where MK-DDC-24 can realistically sell in Year 1-2.
- Sprint 1 focus: VN + TH → SAM = 82,000 + 137,000 = **219,000 units** (~USD 107M)
- Year 3 expansion: +MY → SAM expands to ~**296,000 units** (~USD 145M)

**SOM** (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Realistic share with 1 product line, Year 1-2.
- Year 1 (VN only): 1-3% of Vietnam TAM = **820-2,460 units** (~USD 0.4-1.2M)
- Year 2 (VN+TH): 2-5% of SAM = **4,380-10,950 units** (~USD 2.1-5.4M)
- Year 3 (VN+TH+MY): 3-7% of SAM = **8,880-20,720 units** (~USD 4.4-10.2M)

### 2.6 Cross-Validation with Top-Down

ASEAN BMS Market USD 6.51B × HVAC controls ~35% = USD 2.28B HVAC BMS.
HVAC BMS × DDC controllers ~15% = USD 342M DDC market.
This aligns reasonably with our bottom-up TAM of USD 222.5M for ASEAN-5 (not incl. SG).

## 3. Limitations

- Building stock estimates are extrapolated; precision ±30%
- DDC penetration rate varies significantly by country (TH/MY higher, VN/PH lower)
- Paid reports (BSRIA, MarketsAndMarkets) not accessed due to budget constraints
- Annual unit volumes are stock × replacement + new build; model shows current addressable stock
